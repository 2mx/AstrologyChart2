How it works:

  • Major Only: Uses ASPECTS_DISPLAY: "major".
  • Show All: Includes minor aspects like Semisextile (30°) and Quincunx (150°).
  • Orbs Selection: Switching to Synastry catalog increases the tolerance, causing more aspects to appear for the same planetary positions.
